# Approved image direction

The user's photographic reference is [photo-treatment-reference.png](photo-treatment-reference.png), a moody three-panel harbor image. Its **editing style**, not its city or industrial subjects, controls the look:

- muted olive/teal, green-gray and charcoal; restrained saturation and warm practical glints;
- soft overcast or diffused light, haze, natural texture, and filmic shadow density;
- selective focus; motion blur in background, spray or water when the subject moves, while the vessel remains readable;
- documentary credibility rather than glossy CGI or a synthetic poster look.

Separate three reference roles in every image request: **vessel geometry** (actual product or type reference), **photographic treatment** (the bundled approved style anchor, or a new user reference), and **composition** (space for the logo and bottom headline). Describe which reference controls each property. Do not carry over incidental buildings, birds, ferry, triptych panels, dock, signage, or boat from a style-only reference.

Scene examples are choices, not defaults: speedboat running between low islands; fishing vessel at sea; crew boat approaching a quiet jetty; workboat on a coastal route; active new-build at a clean shipyard. For conceptual vessel artwork, keep proportions, deck layout and operating context plausible. If visual accuracy matters to a specific model, obtain a clear source drawing or photograph. Use the specific source as geometry authority and never claim the generated image documents a completed IMI vessel.
